dbt-for-apache-doris 1.1.0 targets dbt Core 1.12, manages Apache Doris asynchronous materialized views through the standard dbt materialized_view, and covers Table, View, Incremental, Snapshot, Seed, and Data Test workflows. Five business demos show how dbt and Apache Doris handle daily order summaries, late-arriving orders, and more.
